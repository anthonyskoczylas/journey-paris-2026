Some trips end when the plane lands. Others continue for a lifetime. The Journey School International Delegation was born from a simple but ambitious mission — to open doors that many students never imagined they could cross.

Our Mission

We believe international education should never be defined by geography.
It should be defined by opportunity.

Our delegation exists to give students experiences that challenge them academically, connect them globally, and prepare them for the world's leading universities and careers.

Every conference becomes more than an event. It becomes a turning point.

Where It All Began

It started with a small group of students in Playa Hermosa, Costa Rica.

Passionate about leadership, diplomacy, and global issues, what began at a local Model UN soon became something much bigger. Its purpose was singular — to open doors beyond the classroom, expanding young minds and bigger dreams.

Singapore became our first international milestone.

The Next Chapter

Paris 2026

Paris is more than our next destination — it's the continuation of a vision that began in Costa Rica. This conference brings together students from across the world to debate global challenges, exchange perspectives, and build the skills of tomorrow's leaders.

Another opportunity to represent Costa Rica on the international stage.
